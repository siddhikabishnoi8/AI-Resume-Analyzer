Everything you need to know about the ATS parsing engine.

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) score measures how closely your resume aligns with a specific job description. Our score uses a weighted formula: Skill Match (40%), Keyword Match (20%), Experience Alignment (20%), Education (10%), and Layout Formatting (10%) to estimate this rating.
